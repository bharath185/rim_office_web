Performance Appraisal Form

This form has been designed to set the goals & document the progress of the employee throughout the year & also to review the adoption to the Company values.

Guidelines for the review process:

1. Employee has to set goals at the beginning of the year in line with the business plans and discuss with manager.

2. This is applicable for existing employees & new joiners have to set goals within 1 month from the date of joining.

3. Please note that the goals can be moderately appended/modified in October based on the business direction.

4. Manager has to review goal progress once in 3 months & provide continual feedback.

Goals (80%)
  1. SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, Time-bound) to be set by employee.
  2. Number of goals to be set - minimum 3 goals & maximum 5 goals.
  3. Goals can further have multiple measures. For example:
    • Cost per unit (INR)
    • Production cost variance (%)
    • Actual vs budgeted cost variance (INR)
  4. Weightage should be assigned based on the business impact of the objective.
  5. Goals to be finalized in alignment with Manager.
  6. Goals shall be reviewed & rated once in 6 months (April & Oct).

3DCAD Values

At 3DCAD, we believe that the values are the fundamental principles that guide our Organisation. They are the norms, values, attitudes, and practices that form the collective identity of the company. To achieve this, everyone has to inculcate the following values, continuously access and improve the process.

VALUES
  • Integrity
  • Quality
  • Safety
  • Teamwork
BEHAVIOUR (20%)

We trust that the behavior of team members & managers is also a factor, leading to higher productivity and performance by the team and the individual, which should be measured as well.
